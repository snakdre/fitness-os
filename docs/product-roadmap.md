# Fitness OS — Product Roadmap (Executive Summary)

This document provides a high-level product roadmap overview. For the detailed technical roadmap, see [roadmap.md](./roadmap.md).

## Product Mission

Fitness OS is the AI-powered operating system for health and fitness. It removes the friction between tracking, understanding, and acting on your fitness data.

## Core Value Pillars

### 1. Track Everything Effortlessly
Log workouts, meals, and body measurements with minimal friction. AI auto-fills common entries based on your history.

### 2. Understand Your Progress
Beautiful charts and trend analysis turn raw numbers into actionable insight. Know exactly what's working and what isn't.

### 3. AI Coaching at Scale
Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6) provides personalized advice using your actual data — not generic tips. It knows your training history, nutrition patterns, and goals.

### 4. Coach-Client Platform
Professional coaches can manage up to 20 clients in one dashboard, review their data, write programs, and provide feedback — all in one place.

## Key Metrics (v1.0 targets)

- Time to log a workout: < 90 seconds
- Daily active users: 100 by end of month 1
- Onboarding completion rate: > 70%
- Day-7 retention: > 40%
- NPS: > 40
